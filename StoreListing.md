# VisionBill: AI-Powered Smart Receipt Scanner

**Short Description:**
Scan receipts, track pantry prices, and split bills instantly with AI.

**Full Description:**
Transform your messy piles of receipts into actionable financial insights with VisionBill! Using state-of-the-art AI (Gemini 1.5 Flash), VisionBill automatically extracts every item, quantity, and price from your shopping bills with near-perfect accuracy.

**Key Features:**
- 📸 **AI Magic Scanner**: Just point and shoot. VisionBill handles long rolls and multi-segment captures with ease.
- 📉 **Digital Pantry & Price Tracker**: Automatically track price hikes on your daily staples. Know exactly when your favorite milk brand got more expensive!
- 💸 **Instant Bill Splitting**: Split group expenses in seconds. Generate UPI payment links and WhatsApp-ready summaries for your roommates and friends.
- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications**: Get notified the moment the AI finishes processing your long receipts.
- 📑 **Data Portability**: Export your entire spending history to CSV or generate professional PDF receipts for reimbursement.
- 🔒 **Privacy First**: Secure Google authentication and encrypted data storage ensure your financial life stays yours.

**Perfect for:**
- Budget-conscious shoppers tracking inflation.
- Shared households and apartment roommates.
- Frequent diners and social groups.
- Small business owners managing paper expenses.

Stop manual data entry. Start seeing your spending with VisionBill.
